# Security and responsible disclosure policy

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

If you found a vulnerability in tubio, thank you for telling us before making it public.

## How to report

Write to **{{SECURITY_EMAIL}}** with:

- what you found and where (URL, parameter, steps to reproduce),
- the impact you think it has,
- how to reach you.

We reply within **3 business days** and keep you posted until it is fixed. If you like, we credit you in the release notes.

## What we ask

- Only use your own or test accounts; do not access, change or delete other people's data.
- No denial of service, spam, social engineering or physical testing.
- Give us a reasonable time (90 days) to fix it before going public.

If you act in good faith and within these rules, we will not take legal action over your research.

## Scope

tubio.link and its subdomains, and the source code published at {{SOURCE_URL}}. Our customers' own domains and the third-party services they link to (YouTube, Stripe, etc.) are out of scope.
