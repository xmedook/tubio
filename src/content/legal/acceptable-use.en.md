# Acceptable use policy

Do not use tubio for:

- **Fraud or data theft:** pages imitating banks, shops or other services to obtain passwords, cards or personal data (phishing).
- **Malware:** viruses, spyware or deceptive downloads.
- **Spam:** unsolicited bulk messaging or links promoting it.
- **Illegal content:** child exploitation or abuse, terrorism, sale of prohibited goods or services, copyright or trademark infringement.
- **Impersonation:** pretending to be another person, brand or organization.
- **Harassment or hate:** threats, harassment or incitement to violence or discrimination.
- **Explicit sexual content** without enabling the sensitive content warning, and never involving minors.
- **Abusing the service:** overloading it, evading limits or creating accounts in bulk.

If you see something like this, use "Report abuse" at the bottom of any page. We review every report and may disable the link and suspend the account.
