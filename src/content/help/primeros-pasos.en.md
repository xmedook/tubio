# Getting started

1. **Create your account** and confirm your email with the link we send you.
2. In the panel, go to **Links → New → Bio page** and choose the address (for example `tubio.link/yourname`).
3. Add **blocks**: your photo, a heading, buttons to your socials, your WhatsApp, a gallery… Drag them to reorder.
4. In **Design** pick a theme, colors and font. The preview updates when you save.
5. Share your address or download its **QR code**.

Something not working? [Write to us](/help/contact).
