# Plans and payments

- The **Free** plan never expires. Paid plans are billed monthly, yearly or once (lifetime).
- You can **try** a paid plan for free once (you need a confirmed email).
- For now payment is by **bank transfer**: when you pick a plan we give you the reference and the account; upload your receipt and we activate it during business hours. You get a PDF receipt.
- If your plan expires, nothing is deleted: what the Free plan does not include (domains, code blocks, websites, team) pauses until you renew.
