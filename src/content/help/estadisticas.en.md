# Statistics and privacy

You see views, unique visitors and clicks by day, hour, country, city, device, OS, browser, language, referrer and UTM campaign.

**We do not store IP addresses.** A unique visitor is computed from a fingerprint that changes every day, so nobody (us included) can follow a person from one day to the next. Known bots are not counted.

How many days of history you see depends on your plan. You can export to CSV.
