# Your account and your data

- In **Account** you change your name, language, time zone, email and password, and turn on **two-step verification** (authenticator app).
- **Export**: download all your data in one file.
- **Delete account**: removes your account and all its content. It cannot be undone.
- With the Business plan you can invite your **team** (viewer, editor or admin) and create **API keys**.
