# Short links, files and cards

- **Short link**: a `tubio.link/something` address that takes people to another page. Change the destination any time without reprinting.
- **File**: upload a PDF, image or document and it downloads from your address.
- **Contact card (vCard)** and **event (calendar)**: when opened, the phone offers to save the contact or the event.

Each link can have a **password**, **start and end dates**, a **click limit** and **UTM** parameters. Paid plans add pixels, a splash page, destinations by country or device and A/B tests.
