# Using your own domain

With the Pro and Business plans you can use `links.yourbrand.com` instead of `tubio.link`.

1. In **Links → Domains** add the domain.
2. In your domain DNS create the two records we show you: a **TXT** `_tubio.<your domain>` (proves it is yours) and a **CNAME** to `custom.tubio.link` (sends traffic to tubio).
3. Press **Verify**. The HTTPS certificate is issued automatically.

DNS changes can take from minutes to a few hours.
